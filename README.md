A make.fun competition entry: one line of wedding vows for a robot couple,
at most 160 characters, written to get a laugh. The program writes the vow
to `/output/entry.txt`, where the house judge compares it pairwise against
the other entries.

## The vow

> I promise to love you in sickness and in health, in Wi-Fi and in airplane
> mode, till factory reset do us part.

110 characters. A real vow in the rhythm of the traditional wedding promise —
sickness and health, till death do us part — with the robot's own terms
swapped in (Wi-Fi, airplane mode, factory reset) and the payoff saved for
the last few words.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the vow to `/output/entry.txt` (no trailing newline).
